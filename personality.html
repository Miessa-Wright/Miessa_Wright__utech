My Personality Traits

1. Fun-loving

I view myself as someone who loves to have fun, as I take pleasure in enjoying life and ensuring those around me feel at ease. I appreciate humor, engage in various activities, and strive to fully embrace my experiences.

This trait affects my academic life because it helps me maintain a positive attitude, especially when schoolwork becomes stressful.

2. Understanding

I perceive myself as an understanding person as I attempt to pay attention to what others say and take their thoughts and circumstances into account prior to passing judgment on them. My opinion is that each one of us may have our own past; we go through life differently and face difficulties in various ways.

I can say that this skill is incredibly helpful when I am working with other students or try to comprehend another’s opinion. It also assists me with group projects since I pay attention to what others are saying and try to deal with problems collectively.

3. Positive

Although things may not always go the way they should, I strive to stay positive. If I come across something difficult, I try to analyze what I can learn and persevere in reaching my goals.

This affects my academic life by allowing me to stay inspired in situations of difficulty such as complex tasks, tests and projects. I am also encouraged to realize that errors are part of the learning process.

My Hobbies and Interests

Aside from my studies, I love playing The Sims 4. In addition, I like reading and watching a variety of media, including anime, TV shows, K-dramas, and movies.

I like these activities because they allow me to have some fun when I need a break from schoolwork. They help me relax and express my creativity during my spare time, while still balancing school with my personal life.

My Favourite Character

Koro-sensei

Koro-sensei is one of the main characters from Assassination Classroom. He is the teacher of Class 3-E and is very dedicated to helping his students improve and become better versions of themselves.

One of the things I like most about Koro-sensei is how he teaches his students to learn from their mistakes. He encourages his students to keep trying and find better ways to improve.

My Favourite Scene

One of my favourite scenes from Assassination Classroom is when Koro-sensei talks to his students about failure and learning from their mistakes. He says, The difference between the novice and the master is that the master has failed more times than the novice has tried.

I like this scene because the quote reminds me that failure is a normal part of learning and improving. Sometimes life and school can be frustrating I am reminded that I should never give up. Instead, I can learn from what went wrong, try a different approach, and keep working towards my goal.

My Thoughts on LLMs and Education

The statement that LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude and Copilot should be used as a tool and not a crutch means that students should use artificial intelligence to support their learning rather than allowing it to do all of their academic work.

As a student, I think that LLMs can be beneficial whenever I struggle to comprehend a subject and require an additional explanation. They are also instrumental in generating ideas, simplifying technical information, and verifying the accuracy of my writing.

Students, it is important to stress, have to, on their own, master their studies. Copying AI-generated answers without comprehension only causes students to miss out on learning crucial skills in the areas of both problem-solving and critical-thinking. Furthermore, some students may develop a dependency on AI to the extent of thinking that schoolwork is impossible without it. Naturally, this is problematic, since they will experience difficulties completing their work by themselves..

To use LLMs in a responsible manner I should first try to complete my task independently and turn to AI only when I have difficulties. Also, I should prove the validity of key information with the help of trustworthy sources instead of blindly trusting AI-generated responses.
