7 Cures for a Lean Purse

The section “7 Cures for a Lean Purse” from The Richest Man in Babylon emphasizes strategies for enhancing one's financial condition. A key takeaway from this chapter is that simply earning money isn't sufficient; it's essential to understand how to handle the income one receives. The chapter outlines various principles that can assist an individual in becoming more financially accountable.

First Cure

The first remedy is to begin through the method of providing one's purse with a steady amount of cash to save up some portion of your money earned. It involves having a saving habit, as opposed to saving money only when there is anything left over. For me personally, I can practice this way of saving by setting aside some percentage of any payment I receive even if the sum of money is small.

Second Cure

The second remedy is to manage my spending. This means recognizing the difference between wanting something and needing something. It is very easy to use my money on things that I don’t really need. I will take this advice in the future and will have a budget for my spending and will not spend without necessity. This way I will have more money for essential things like education, emergencies and future aims.

Third Cure

The third remedy is to multiply your gold. To me, this means making my savings work for me instead of just letting the savings sit idle. In the future, I could further learn about investing and multiplying my money. But I will also need to understand the risks involved before I start putting my money into anything.

Fourth Cure

The fourth remedy is keeping one’s resources safe from being taken away. This can involve being careful about where to put one’s finances and avoiding making decisions recklessly when one does not have full understanding of the circumstances surrounding that decision. For the author, having this guidance is essential, as the desire to become rich fast may lead to making unwise choices. Instead, the author should do research and consult reliable experts before investing money into something.

Fifth Cure

The fifth solution involves transforming your home into a source of income. This entails discovering ways to ensure that your housing and living expenses work in your favor instead of becoming a financial burden. Once I'm in a position to buy my own house, I will ensure that my choices align with my income needs and future financial goals.

Sixth Cure

The sixth cure consists of making sure that you have an income in the future. This entails making financial preparations for the future, rather than only focusing on the present. As a university student, it is possible for me to start preparing, by improving my skill set and aiming for a job that helps me earn a healthy income. I could also adopt sound saving and financial practices now so that I am better prepared later.

Seventh Cure

The seventh healing is to improve your earning potential. I found this point interesting because it shows that improvement of oneself can also correspond to a rise in his or her wealth. As a student taking a degree in Computer Networking and Security, I can work on improving my potential by gaining technical competencies, qualifications, experience and information. Being consistent in learning will prepare me for good job possibilities in the IT field.

This chapter has made me realize that achieving a good financial position is never an instantaneous process. It needs commitment, suitable preparation and right mindset. I can begin to put this lesson into practice immediately by getting my financial house in order and saving and investing in myself.

5 Laws of Gold

My main message from the Richest Man in Babylon is that achieving financial prosperity may not depend on the money that you earn, but on the habits you develop and your discipline as a person. It is possible to earn quite a sum of money and remain broke, due to a person's tendency to spend every dollar they receive. On the contrary, a person can acquire some saving habits and discipline in spending their money so that finances will have a solid base.

First Law

The first principle states that a person who saves at least somewhat of his earnings will definitely be visited by money. This relates to the first cure from the previous chapter. For me, it refers to the fact that saving should be made a habit. There is no need to wait for a big amount of money before starting to save money.

Second Law

The second law implies that money should be put to good use so as to make sure that it increases its amount. I interpret this as meaning that having money saved in itself is not enough for effective long-term growth. In the future, I intend to find out more about responsibl e investing and discover various ways of making my money generate income while being aware of possible risks.

Third Law

The significance of using money as knowledge is emphasized in point three. I thus discovered that I shouldn't trust anyone who promises to make me wealthy. When I want to invest my money, I have to do some research and make sure the company or individual I'm investing in is trustworthy.

Fourth Law

The Fourth Law warns against investing in things that one does not understand. This information is important, in my opinion, because people frequently make financial decisions based on enthusiasm or the prospect of making large earnings in the near future. In my situation, I would prefer to learn about investments before making any purchases. I must first educate myself if I do not understand what I am going to invest in.

Fifth Law

There is a warning provided by the fourth law against the person investing things that the individual does not grasp. I believe that this piece of information is crucial because it is quite often the case that people may make financial decisions due to some excitement or the promises of receiving high profits shortly. In my case, I would like to understand investment before spending my money on it. If I do not have the understanding of what I am about to invest, I need to learn about it first.

My Biggest Takeaway

My main message from the Richest Man in Babylon is that achieving financial prosperity may not depend on the money that you earn, but on the habits you develop and your discipline as a person. It is possible to earn quite a sum of money and remain broke, due to a person's tendency to spend every dollar they receive. On the contrary, a person can acquire some saving habits and discipline in spending their money so that finances will have a solid base. Both chapters have made me reflect on the management of my money and preparing for the future. Although I do not currently have a significant financial income due to being a student, I can still establish good practices for my future. I can begin by coming up with a budget, limiting unnecessary expenses and saving a portion of my income. Another way to implement the teachings from these two chapters is through investing in myself. My education as well as technical expertise can help me in earning more money in future. Since I am studying Computer Networking and Security at the moment, I can work on getting certifications, doing hands-on projects and obtaining skills that an employer expects. I want to be financially literate. Instead of being guided solely by the opinions of others, I can spend time on researching such areas of the money management field as budgeting, investing and financial planning.
